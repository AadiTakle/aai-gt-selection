# Assumptions and Evidence Register

## Evidence classes

- **Verified research finding:** Supported by a checked primary or strong secondary source.
- **Independent context:** Reported by an independent source, but not proof of program impact.
- **Company claim:** Statement made by GT School, Alpha School, or an affiliated party.
- **Reasoned inference:** Synthesis that must not be presented as a source's direct finding.
- **Open assumption:** Unverified condition that must be tested before dependent work proceeds.

Do not silently promote a claim from one class to another.

## Update procedure

When adding or changing an entry:

1. Cite the exact source or validation artifact.
2. Record whether the claim describes the current GT context or general research.
3. State what downstream requirement, decision, or work item depends on it.
4. Update the traceability matrix when the evidence changes product scope or acceptance.
5. Add a decision-log entry if the change alters a requirement or chosen direction.

## Evidence-use rules

- Company claims may define what is being tested, not prove that it is true.
- General research does not establish GT-specific feasibility.
- Absence of public evidence is not evidence that no private evidence exists.
- Correlation and predictive validity do not establish program impact.
- Weak or unresolved evidence must narrow the claim, not be hidden.
