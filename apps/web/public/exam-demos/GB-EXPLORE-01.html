How to use — Explorer's Map

Use the arrow keys or the arrow buttons to walk around the foggy map. The fog lifts only near you, so walk until you have found every named landmark. Then the fog comes back: turn the dial to point at the landmark that is named on screen and press the check. Last, walk home again through the fog by the shortest way you can.
