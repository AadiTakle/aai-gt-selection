How to use — Shape-Fill Form Board

Drag a tray piece onto the outline to place it. Tap a piece while it is still in the tray to turn it. Fill the whole outline with no gaps — some tray pieces are extra and will not fit. Tap a placed piece to pick it back up. Press done when the board is full.

Waiting for item
Waiting for an item from the host…
Drag a piece onto the outline · tap a tray piece to turn it · tap a placed piece to remove it
Keyboard: Tab to a piece · Enter turns it · G picks it up · arrows move · Enter drops · Esc puts it back

The board is ready. Press play when you are, and it starts then.
