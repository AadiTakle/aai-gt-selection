How to use — Shape Smith

Fill the whole outline with pieces from the tray. Tap a piece to pick it, tap turn (or flip, when it is offered) to change how it sits, then tap a square in the outline to drop it. Tap a piece you already dropped to take it back. Some tray pieces do not belong. Use as few turns and drops as you can, then press DONE.
