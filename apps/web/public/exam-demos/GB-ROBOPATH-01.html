How to use — Path Coder

Build one program that drives the robot to the door and over every key. Tap the arrow buttons to add steps: forward, turn left, turn right. Turns are from the robot's own point of view. Tap a step in the strip to delete it. Press RUN to watch the whole program play, then press SUBMIT to send your program.
