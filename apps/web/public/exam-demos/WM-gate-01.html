How to use — Gatekeeper

Watch what goes past the gate. At surprise checkpoints the gate says STOP and asks you a question about what just went by — answer it by tapping, using undo to fix a tap. The parade then keeps going and stops again later, so stay ready the whole way through. Take as long as you need at each checkpoint.
