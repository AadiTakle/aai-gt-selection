How to use — Sentence Sense

Tap a mark in the sentence line to choose where the next word goes, then tap a word card to put it there. The little words (a, an, the) can go in as often as you like. Build the sentence that makes sense — some other orders follow the rules of grammar but describe something impossible. Tap a word in the line to take it back out, and press the check (✓) when every card is in.
