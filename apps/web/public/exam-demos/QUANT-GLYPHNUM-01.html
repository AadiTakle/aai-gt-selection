How to use — Alien Numbers

The machine writes numbers with marks nobody has explained. The writing at the top is one number. The line below runs from nothing on the left up to the number written at its right-hand end. Tap the one place out of five where the writing belongs. One tap sends your answer. Nobody tells you what a mark is worth — you work that out by watching where the machine puts things.
