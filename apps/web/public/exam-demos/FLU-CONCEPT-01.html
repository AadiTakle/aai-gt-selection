How to use — Mystery Gate

The gate opens only for figures that follow a secret rule. Build a figure with the buttons and press send (→) to test it: openers go to the Opens tray, the rest to the Stays shut tray. You get a limited number of tests. When you think you know the rule, press I know it and say whether each new figure opens the gate.
