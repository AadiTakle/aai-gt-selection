How to use — Claim Duel

Read the claim, then read the question above the cards. Use Add to put any cards you think help into your case, and use Strongest to mark the single best one. Press the check (✓) to send it. A rival idea appears next: mark the one card that shows the rival is wrong, then press the check again.
