How to use — Check It Twice

The sorter already filled these groups, and some tiles are in the wrong one. Each group header shows its pattern. Tap a tile to pick it up, then tap the group it belongs in to move it. Tap the small check (✓) on a tile to mark that you looked at it. You may press Turn it in at any time — right away, or after you have checked everything.
