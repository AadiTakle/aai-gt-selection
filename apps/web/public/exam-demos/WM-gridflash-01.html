How to use — Star Grid

Stars flash on the grid all at once, then go dark. Some rounds bring one star back and ask if its colour is the SAME or CHANGED — use the buttons or the left and right arrow keys. Other rounds ask you to tap every square that had a star; the order does not matter. Take as long as you need.
