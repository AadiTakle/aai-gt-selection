How to use — Hanging Mobile

Every arm of this mobile hangs level. The same figure always weighs the same, and a load on a short arm has to be heavier than a load on a long arm. One hook is empty. Tap the group of figures that would keep every arm level, then press the check (✓) to send your answer.
