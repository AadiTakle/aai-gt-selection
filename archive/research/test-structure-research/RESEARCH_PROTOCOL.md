# Test-Structure BrainLift — Research Fleet Protocol

Parallel DOK-1/DOK-2 research for a K–8 **automated adaptive screener** (R11; D-015 automated/
reproducible, D-016 adaptive, D-017 text-only reading-as-capability). Developed to be **merged
back into `gifted-assessment-quality-brainlift/` later**, so it uses the same Knowledge-Tree/DOK
conventions and **cross-references** that brainlift's 10.x/13.x instead of duplicating them.

## Waves
- **Wave 1 (breadth):** one agent per Category A–I → writes `research-shards/cat_<X>.md`.
- **Merge:** parent merges shards into `brainlift-test-structure.md` (Knowledge Tree) +
  `source-register-annotated.md`, then reads for gaps/overlaps.
- **Wave 2 (depth/fill):** targeted agents deepen thin cells or resolve conflicting facts.
- **Handoff:** DOK 1/2 + `source-register` complete; parent poses DOK 3 provocation questions.
  **DOK 3/4 remain the author's to own** — the fleet never writes insights or stances.

## Shard format (each agent, one file, write-only-your-own)
1. `## DOK 1 — Facts` under titled Subcategories; every fact cites source + flags (COI / [UNVERIFIED] / [ADJACENT] / [gap]).
2. `## DOK 2 — Summary` (3–6 sentences, each traceable to a DOK-1 fact).
3. `## Source register entries` table: Source | DOI/URL | Tier (T1 peer/meta · T2 Standards/canonical · T3 proponent/vendor · T4 practitioner) | Flags | one-line fact.
4. `## Cross-references` to gifted-assessment 10.x/13.x (cite, don't duplicate).
5. `## Product linkage (R11 screener)` — 2–4 concrete structural/config implications.
6. `## Tensions / disagreements` — conflicting findings (DOK 3 seeds). No DOK 3/4 content.

## Categories
A adaptive structure & routing · B item ordering/position/context · C retakes & repeated administration ·
D presentation & interaction · E cognitive load & working memory · F session design & developmental fit ·
G motivation/effort/engagement structure · H fairness & access in structure · I auditability & reproducibility of structure.
