How to use — Proof Hunt

Press play when you are ready — the story appears then. Step 1: read the story and tap the best answer to the question (or press its number key). Step 2: the choices tuck away, the story lights up and asks you for the proof — tap the one sentence that proves your answer. It turns green, and tapping another sentence moves the proof. Change answer brings the choices back. When you have both, press the check (✓). You need the right answer and the right proof.
