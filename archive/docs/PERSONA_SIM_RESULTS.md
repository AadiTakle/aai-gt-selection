# Persona Simulator — Representative Recovery Run (SYNTHETIC)

**Status:** research evidence, not ratified product direction. Born-synthetic
(`synthetic_only: true`, `validated: false`; D-006, R9). Implements the "smallest
vertical slice" of `research/test-structure-research/PERSONA_SIM_AND_VALIDATION_DESIGN.md` §1.

> **CLAIM BOUNDARY — READ BEFORE QUOTING ANY NUMBER.** Personas were generated by the
> 3PL in `packages/cat-engine/src/irt.ts` and then scored by the estimator that inverts
> that same 3PL. **Theta recovery here is CIRCULAR.** These numbers evidence exactly
> three things: (a) the session → scoring → estimation path is wired correctly,
> (b) the estimator's bias and precision under known truth, and (c) how many items a
> target SE requires. They are **not** evidence that any metric measures real ability,
> **not** convergent or predictive validity, and **not** program impact. Access,
> reliability, outcome change, and causal impact are separate milestones (R10). Tuning
> weights on this data tunes to the simulator's assumptions, not to children.

## Run configuration

| setting | value |
|---|---|
| personas | 1000 (50% unconstrained population, 10% each of the five archetypes) |
| seed | `persona-sim-2026-07-27` |
| bank | 84 born-synthetic keyed single-select items: 15 standing + 6 effort rungs × 4 domains |
| response model | 3PL, `a ~ U(0.9, 1.6)`, `b = (rung − 10.5) / 3`, `c = 0.25` (four options) |
| arms | `FixedSequencer` (all 84 items) and `TwoStageSequencer` (cap 8 standing + 4 effort per domain) |
| screening cut | theta ≥ 1.2816 (90th percentile of N(0,1)); a band, never an admission decision |
| rapid-guess RT floor | 1500 ms |

## Headline result: the scoring path is correct

**Production-scoring vs generator mismatches: 0 of 84,000 trials in the fixed arm and
0 of 29,100 in the two-stage arm.** Every persona emitted a raw option index; the real
server-side rule resolved correctness against the bank key; the resolved accuracy
matched the simulated response in every case. This is the one non-circular check in the
study, and it is what makes the recovery numbers below interpretable at all.

## Composite theta recovery (fixed arm, 60 standing items)

| condition | RMSE | bias | Pearson r | mean SE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EAP · RT gate (production path) | 0.467 | −0.199 | 0.894 | 0.388 |
| MLE · RT gate | 0.499 | −0.166 | 0.888 | — |
| EAP · no gate | 0.961 | −0.656 | 0.696 | — |
| EAP · perfect engagement telemetry | 0.396 | −0.066 | 0.911 | 0.394 |

Two readings matter.

1. **The reported SE is honest.** Under perfect engagement telemetry, RMSE (0.396)
   matches the mean posterior SE (0.394). The estimator's own uncertainty statement is
   calibrated against known truth, and residual bias is −0.066 — consistent with normal
   EAP shrinkage toward the prior in a cohort whose mean is +0.29.
2. **The RT-based rapid-guess filter is necessary but not sufficient.** It cuts RMSE from
   0.961 to 0.467, but a residual −0.199 bias survives it, because the filter can only
   catch *fast* disengagement. A slow, checked-out response still enters theta scored at
   the guessing floor. Closing that gap needs real engagement telemetry (M-ENGAGE), not a
   tighter RT threshold.

Per-domain (15 items each) recovery is RMSE 0.73–0.75 with mean SE 0.64–0.66 — a single
domain read off 15 items is a weak estimate, whatever the composite looks like.

## Items needed for a target SE (fixed arm, pooled across domains)

| target mean SE | administered standing items |
|---|---|
| 0.50 | 32 |
| 0.45 | 39 |
| 0.40 | 52 |
| 0.35 / 0.30 / 0.25 | not reached with 60 items (best: 0.388 at k=60) |

Per domain, 15 items reach only SE ≈ 0.65; no per-domain target at or below 0.50 is
reached. The x-axis is **administered** items, not surviving items, so the curve
includes the precision lost to the engagement gate rather than hiding it.

**Implication for form length:** with `c = 0.25` four-option items and mid-range
discrimination, a per-domain SE of 0.5 is not reachable inside a short form. Either the
composite is the reportable unit, or the item pool needs higher discrimination / lower
guessing (more options, constructed response), or the form gets much longer.

## Two-stage arm: the honest cost of a short form

| arm | mean items | composite RMSE (gated) | Pearson r | mean SE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fixed (84 items) | 84.0 | 0.467 | 0.894 | 0.388 |
| two-stage (bracketed) | 29.1 | 0.640 | 0.753 | 0.586 |

The two-stage bracket stops each domain after ~3 standing probes, which is by design
(anti-ramp, precision stop) but buys a materially noisier theta. Fixed and two-stage
agree on 91.0% of cut decisions (kappa 0.351). That disagreement is a *precision*
consequence of form length, not evidence that either structure is more valid.

## Classification at the cut (fixed arm)

| condition | accuracy | kappa | sensitivity | false-negative rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EAP · RT gate | 0.886 | 0.458 | 0.377 | 0.623 |
| EAP · no gate | 0.857 | 0.185 | 0.126 | 0.874 |
| EAP · perfect engagement telemetry | 0.889 | 0.547 | 0.547 | 0.453 |
| EAP · RT gate, cut matched on the estimate | 0.886 | 0.574 | 0.642 | 0.358 |

**The false-negative rates here are high and must not be waved away.** Two separate
causes, both actionable:

- **Cut definition.** EAP shrinks toward the prior, so applying the *true-theta*
  threshold directly to theta-hat under-selects by construction. Matching the cut on the
  estimate's own distribution keeps the same selected share and drops false negatives
  from 99 to 57 children per 1000. A cut is policy (D-016) and must be defined on the
  scale it is applied to.
- **Measurement error near the cut.** Even at best, roughly a third of truly above-cut
  personas are missed. A single screening pass at this precision cannot be the only route
  in; a retry/defer band and an alternative referral path are load-bearing, not optional.

## Engagement gate and archetypes (fixed arm)

| archetype | RMSE (gated) | RMSE (no gate) | RMSE (perfect telemetry) | mean effort-valid rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| population | 0.458 | 0.749 | 0.410 | 0.864 |
| high_standing_low_learning | 0.517 | 1.066 | 0.398 | 0.864 |
| moderate_standing_high_learning | 0.392 | 0.785 | 0.345 | 0.850 |
| high_ability_low_effort | 0.584 | 1.873 | 0.432 | 0.655 |
| high_both | 0.515 | 1.001 | 0.401 | 0.875 |
| low_both | 0.324 | 0.408 | 0.325 | 0.879 |

The able-but-disengaged archetype is exactly where an ungated score fails worst
(RMSE 1.873 → 0.584 with the gate → 0.432 with perfect telemetry). This is the intended
stress test: without the gate, low effort is read as low ability.

## Learning-rate channel: a NULL, and specifically a POWER null

| lambda estimator | Pearson r | mean slope |
|---|---|---|
| raw OLS score slope over the novel block | 0.109 | −0.0686 |
| slope of the 3PL residual (difficulty removed) | 0.085 | +0.0344 |

The OLS within-session learning rate did **not** recover injected lambda. Two things are
visible and both are design findings:

1. The novel block is administered at **ascending difficulty**, so a raw score slope
   conflates the learning climb with difficulty escalation — that is the negative mean
   raw slope. Residualizing against the 3PL expectation flips the mean slope positive,
   which is the climb.
2. Removing that confound does **not** rescue the per-persona correlation. The binding
   constraint is trial count: lambda ≈ 0.03 theta/trial over a 6-item block moves the
   expected score by a few hundredths against a per-persona slope SD an order of
   magnitude larger.

**A within-session learning rate is not recoverable from a block this short**, and no
amount of weight-tuning changes that. `M-LEARNRATE` remains a labelled hypothesis
(H-series), not a validated metric. Anyone proposing to weight it in a composite needs a
much longer novel block at held-constant difficulty first.

## Validation-harness wiring (proven end-to-end)

The runner emits `persona_sim_scores.csv` + manifest in the exact schema
`scripts/validation-harness` consumes. The harness's fail-closed born-synthetic guard
passed all four checks plus the SHA-256 integrity commitment, and every analysis ran:
Cronbach's alpha 0.779 over 60 items, SEM 3.43, 33.8% of the cohort inside the
±1.96×SEM ambiguous band at the cut, no DIF items flagged B/C on the purified pass, and
0.1% at the maximum score (no ceiling compression).

**The harness's `cogat_sas` / `map_rit` columns are SYNTHETIC PROXIES** generated from the
same latent theta as the item responses. They are circular with the new-test score by
construction. The concurrent-validity and incremental-validity numbers in that report
demonstrate the *method*; they are not convergent validity and not predictive validity.
Real external substantiation requires real CogAT/MAP under a formal authorized live-data
decision (B-06), which has not been made.

## Open assumptions a reviewer must check

Every one of these is an **[A] open assumption**, not calibrated from data. Cohort-level
numbers are properties of these choices.

1. **Trait correlation matrix** (`latent.ts`): the ability positive manifold (r ≈ 0.4–0.6)
   and the deliberately weak learning-rate/ability coupling (r ≈ 0.2–0.3). The
   standing↔learning-rate separation the two-regime hypothesis needs is *assumed here*,
   not demonstrated.
2. **Engagement scale**: base on-task probability ≈ 0.94 for an average persona, drift of
   0.06 per off-task trial capped at a 0.15 total decline. The cap is required — without
   it a long form death-spirals, which is an artefact of an unbounded model.
3. **Rapid-guess propensity** (~0.75 of off-task trials) and the 1500 ms RT floor. These
   set how much disengagement the RT filter can see, and therefore the residual bias.
4. **RT model**: log-normal, median 9 s at ability, +0.28 log-ms per theta unit of
   `(b − theta)`, 4% lapse rate. No real RT distribution informed these.
5. **Item parameters**: `a ~ U(0.9, 1.6)`, `c = 0.25`. Guessing dominates the precision
   result; a different option count changes every SE number above.
6. **The dial→theta mapping** `theta = (dial − 10.5) / 3` treats an ordinal design rung as
   a calibrated difficulty. It is a simulator convenience, not a calibration.
7. **Lambda scale** (0.03 ± 0.03 theta/trial). The power null above is relative to this
   scale; a larger true lambda would be easier to detect.

## Scope

**In scope (this slice):** persona latent generator, the accuracy family (M-ACC,
M-DIFFREACH, M-ERRTYPE/M-LURETYPE), the RT family with lapses, the engagement gate, the
