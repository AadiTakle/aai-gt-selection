## 3. The persona simulator works

`packages/cat-engine/src/persona-sim/` + `scripts/persona-sim/`. Seeded, pure,
dependency-free. Full numbers in `docs/PERSONA_SIM_RESULTS.md`; reproduced in this
session with seed `persona-sim-2026-07-27` **[V]**.

**The one non-circular result: 0 scoring mismatches across 113,100 trials.** The
keyed scoring path never disagreed with the simulated response. That is what this
exercise was for.

Fixed arm (60 standing items), composite theta **[V]**:

| condition | RMSE | bias | r | mean SE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EAP · RT gate (production) | 0.467 | −0.199 | 0.894 | 0.388 |
| EAP · no gate | 0.961 | −0.656 | 0.696 | — |
| EAP · perfect engagement telemetry | 0.396 | −0.066 | 0.911 | 0.394 |

The estimator is **calibrated**: under perfect engagement telemetry, RMSE (0.396)
equals its own mean posterior SE (0.394).

**Items for a target SE, pooled [V]:** 32 → 0.50 · 39 → 0.45 · 52 → 0.40. SE 0.35
and below is **not reachable** at 60 items. **No per-domain SE at or below 0.50 is
reachable with 15 items** (best ≈ 0.65). With four-option items (`c = 0.25`), the
composite is the reportable unit — a per-domain score needs a longer form or a
higher-discrimination pool. **[I]**

### Two results that must not be buried

1. **The RT rapid-guess filter is necessary but not sufficient.** It cuts RMSE from
   0.961 to 0.467, but a −0.199 downward bias survives, because RT sees only *fast*
   disengagement. A slow checked-out response still enters theta near the guessing
   floor. Fixing it needs real `M-ENGAGE` telemetry, not a tighter threshold. **[V]**
2. **The within-session learning rate is a NULL — specifically a POWER null.** OLS
   slope vs injected lambda: r = 0.109 raw, 0.085 after removing the
   ascending-difficulty confound. Lambda ≈ 0.03 theta/trial over a 6-item block moves
   expected score by hundredths against a per-persona slope SD an order of magnitude
   larger. **A learning rate is not recoverable from a block this short.**
   `M-LEARNRATE` stays a labelled hypothesis, not a metric to weight. **[V]**

False-negative rate at the cut is 0.623 on the production path; part is a policy
artefact (applying a true-theta threshold to a shrunk estimate under-selects), and
matching the cut on the estimate's own distribution drops false negatives from 99 to
57 per 1000. The remainder is genuine error near the cut, which means **one screening
pass at this precision cannot be the only route in**. **[I]**

### What the simulator cannot tell you

Recovering theta from the same 3PL that generated the responses is **circular**. This
establishes code correctness, estimator bias/precision, and item-count requirements.
It is not evidence that any metric measures real ability, not convergent or predictive
validity, and not program impact (R10). Tuning weights on this data tunes to the
simulator's assumptions.

Seven open assumptions **[A]** are listed at the end of `docs/PERSONA_SIM_RESULTS.md`.
The load-bearing one: the trait correlation matrix **assumes** weak learning-rate/
ability coupling (r ≈ 0.2–0.3). The standing-vs-learning-rate separation the two-regime
design depends on is assumed there, not demonstrated.
