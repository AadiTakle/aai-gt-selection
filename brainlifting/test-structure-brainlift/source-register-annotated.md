# Source Register (annotated) — Test Structure BrainLift

**Status:** SCAFFOLD. Populated during DOK 1 research. Companion to `brainlift-test-structure.md`.

Every load-bearing DOK 1 fact traces to an entry here. Each entry is tiered and COI-flagged so
the brainlift can separate strong evidence from weak/vendor/practitioner claims.

## Tiering (same scheme as the other brainlifts)

- **T1 — Peer-reviewed empirical / meta-analysis** (strongest).
- **T2 — Standards & canonical texts** (AERA/APA/NCME 2014; foundational psychometric/CLT texts).
- **T3 — Author/vendor-affiliated or proponent-generated** (flag COI).
- **T4 — Practitioner / editorial / non-peer-reviewed** (heuristic only; validate before load-bearing use).
- Flags: `[COI]`, `[UNVERIFIED]`, `[ADJACENT]` (evidence from an adjacent domain, e.g., adult selection or medical decision-making), `[gap]`.
