How to use — Ranking Ladder

Each clue card shows two characters: the one on the left ranks higher than the one on the right. Put them together to work out the whole order. Then tap the choice below whose ladder (top is highest) matches, and press the check (✓) to send your answer. The machine notes your answer and brings the next puzzle.
