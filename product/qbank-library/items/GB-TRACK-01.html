How to use — Firefly Jars

Some jars light up because a firefly flew in. The lids close and the jars slide around and swap places. You cannot tap while they are moving. When they stop, tap every jar you think still holds a firefly (tap again to unpick it) and press DONE. There is no clock — take as long as you need.
