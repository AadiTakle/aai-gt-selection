How to use — What the Robot Sees

Imagine standing where the robot stands and looking the way it looks. Tap the cards in the order the robot sees them, from the robot's left to its right. Harder scenes have walls in the way: anything the robot cannot see past a wall is left out, but it can see through a glass window set into one. There is one empty space for each thing the robot can see. Press Undo to take the last card back, then press the check (✓) to send.
