How to use — Alien Numbers

The machine writes numbers with marks nobody has explained. Each mark is one digit, and the mark on the left counts for the most. The line below runs from nothing on the left up to the number written at its right-hand end. Slide the handle to where you think the writing belongs, then press Send. Nobody tells you what a mark is worth, how the marks combine, or how many marks the machine has — you work that out by watching where it puts things.
