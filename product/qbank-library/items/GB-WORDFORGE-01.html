How to use — Word Forge

Tap letter tiles to spell a word into the slot, then press the check (✓) to forge it and start the next one. Build as many real words as you can before the timer runs out. A tile can be used again in a different word. Use clear (⌫) to empty the slot, or DONE to finish early. Only try words you think are real: every two made-up words take away one of your real ones.
