How to use — Path Connect

Tap each tile to rotate its pipe a quarter-turn, and connect a road from the start marker to every goal marker (passing through any gems) in as few turns as possible. Harder items have several goals, so the road has to fork through T-shaped pipes. It locks on its own when the road reaches them all.
