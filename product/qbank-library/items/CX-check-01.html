How to use — Check It Twice

The sorter already filled these groups, and some tiles are in the wrong one. Each group header shows its pattern. Every tile has a grip (⋮⋮) and can be moved: tap a tile to pick it up, then tap one of the dashed groups to drop it there. Tap the small box on a tile to mark that you looked at it, and tap it again to clear the mark. You may press Turn it in at any time — right away, or after you have checked everything.
