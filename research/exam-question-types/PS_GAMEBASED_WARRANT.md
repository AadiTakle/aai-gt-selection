# Processing-Speed & Game-Based Warrant

*Research memo — should "processing speed" be a testable domain/type family, and is "game-based" a distinct cognitive construct?*

Serves **H4** ("broaden who can demonstrate ability") and decision **D-015** (fully-automated, adaptive, no-human screener whose north star is Timeback-fit). Requirement/evidence mappings beyond H4/D-015 are inherited from the orchestrator's recat plan and not reopened here.

Claim labels used below: **[Verified finding]** = supported by cited peer-reviewed evidence; **[Independent context]** = accepted background not specific to GT; **[Reasoned inference]** = my logic from the evidence; **[Open assumption]** = unverified, needs local data. Reminders per guardrails: *predictive validity ≠ program impact*, and *correlation ≠ incremental validity*.

---

## TL;DR — Recommendation: **B**

Cut the dedicated processing-speed (PS) item types and do **not** create a `processing_speed` area. Retain speed only as **cross-cutting, engagement-gated efficiency signals** (`M-RT`, `M-COMBO`, `M-SPEEDACC`, each gated by `M-ENGAGE`) plus **consistency signals** (`M-RTVAR`, `M-LAPSE`) computed on the reasoning items the child is already answering. "Game-based" is confirmed to be a *delivery/measurement layer*, not a construct, so `GB-*` remain wrappers over the four domains and never a domain of their own. The high-tail g-loading, gifted-profile, and equity evidence — combined with D-015's Timeback-fit north star and the engagement-gate SPOV — make a standalone clerical-speed probe (Blink Towers, Beat the Bar, Secret Decoder, Difference Detectives) a net liability for *this* screener.

---

## Q1 — Does processing speed (Gs) add *incremental* validity to gifted identification beyond g / Gf?

**CHC placement and g-loading.** In Cattell–Horn–Carroll theory, Processing Speed (Gs) is a broad Stratum-II ability defined as the speed/fluency of executing *simple, over-learned* cognitive tasks (perceptual speed, rate-of-test-taking, number facility). CHC treats Gs as of *secondary importance relative to Gf and Gc* during the learning phase of skill acquisition, and Gs indicators load *lower on g* than fluid-reasoning or comprehension-knowledge indicators. **[Verified finding]** (McGrew, 2009 [1]). This is the structural reason Gs is a thin proxy for the general ability GT identification targets. **[Reasoned inference]**

**Gifted-profile evidence (the high tail).** In gifted and high-ability samples the Processing Speed Index (PSI) is repeatedly the *lowest* index, and its relationship to overall ability weakens at the top of the distribution. In a multi-site sample of 390 gifted, highly-gifted, and twice-exceptional (2e) children, WISC-V means ranged from "very superior" on untimed high-g Verbal Comprehension down to *average* on "low-g Processing Speed (irrelevant to gifted identification)," with ≥1.5 SD index discrepancies in a majority of cases — undermining Full-Scale IQ and prompting the recommendation to identify on high-g/reasoning composites instead. The same pattern held in an earlier 334-child WISC-IV gifted sample. **[Verified finding]** (Gilman et al., 2026 [2]; NAGC, 2018 [3]). A descriptive Wechsler study similarly found the General Ability Index — which excludes PS and working memory — best matched gifted children's potential, with most scoring only average/above-average on PSI. **[Verified finding]** (Pezzuti et al., 2022 [4]).

Critically, clinical–neuropsychological analysis argues the depressed PS in gifted children is largely a *statistical/behavioral artifact* — perfectionism, self-monitoring, and aversion to timed clerical paper-and-pencil tasks — **not** a true neurocognitive speed deficit. **[Verified finding]** (Beljan, Gardner, & Homaijani, 2024 [5]). So low PSI in this population usually reflects construct-irrelevant behavior, not lower ability.

**Does clerical Gs predict high-end achievement/acceleration beyond Gf/Gc?** The strongest pro-speed result is that, in a *general* child sample, Woodcock–Johnson Processing-Speed + Working-Memory clusters carried incremental validity for broad achievement over Fluid Reasoning. **[Verified finding]** (Luo, Thompson, & Detterman, 2006 [6]). But that is a general-population achievement result, not a high-tail acceleration result — *predictive validity in the middle of the distribution is not evidence for gifted selection*, and the incremental variance is shared "efficiency," not clerical speed per se. **[Reasoned inference]** When ability level is modeled directly, it is *reasoning/specific abilities* — not clerical speed — that gain incremental validity in the *high-IQ* group. **[Verified finding]** (Breit & Preckel, 2020 [7]). Net: clerical Gs does not earn a dedicated slot for identifying children who can accelerate. **[Reasoned inference]**

**Measurement risk / equity (H4).** Timed clerical/perceptual-speed tasks import construct-irrelevant variance that disproportionately penalizes exactly the children H4 exists to protect. Including PS in the composite "may lower the Full Scale score below cutoffs for gifted identification" and "can block [2e children's] access to gifted programs." **[Verified finding]** (NAGC, 2018 [3]). In gifted-with-ADHD children the GAI-vs-PSI/working-memory gaps run roughly *twice* as large as in ADHD alone, and 2e masking is bidirectional and heterogeneous. **[Verified finding]** (Cornoldi et al., 2023 [8]; Rizzo, Pinnelli, & Minnaert, 2025 [9]). A speeded battery therefore risks *systematically hiding* able 2e, ADHD, motor-slow, and anxious children — a direct conflict with H4 and with a no-human screener that cannot exercise clinical judgment to discount a low PSI. **[Reasoned inference]**

**Counter-evidence — the legitimate role of speed.** Speed is not noise. Mental speed correlates with intelligence: a 50-year meta-analysis (172 studies, 53,542 participants) found a modest but robust mean speed–IQ correlation (~|.24|) that *strengthens as task complexity rises*, and mental speed tends to relate more to Gf than Gc. **[Verified finding]** (Sheppard & Vernon, 2008 [10]). Crucially, the *diagnostic* part of the response-time distribution is the **slow tail**: under the "worst-performance rule," an individual's *slowest* responses predict ability better than their fastest or average ones. **[Verified finding]** (Larson & Alderton, 1990 [11]). A meta-analysis (19 studies / 3,767 participants) confirmed this, with correlations rising across the distribution (fastest ≈ −.18, mean ≈ −.28, slowest ≈ −.33) — i.e., *lapses/inconsistency* carry the ability signal, not peak speed. **[Verified finding]** (Schubert, 2019 [12]).

This is the key distinction the brief asks for: **RT/consistency signals ≠ clerical processing-speed subtests.** The ability-relevant speed information (worst-performance, intra-individual variability, absence of lapses) is a *by-product of the reasoning items already administered*; it does not require Blink-Towers-style clerical tasks, which measure the low-g perceptual-speed narrow abilities most exposed to equity artifacts. **[Reasoned inference]** Two further points make this decisive for a no-human screener: (a) the slow-tail signal is contaminated by disengagement, and rapid *fast* responses are frequently **rapid-guessing** — a low-effort behavior detectable from item response time and distinct from genuine "solution behavior." **[Verified finding]** (Wise & Kong, 2005 [13]). Hence "fast" is ambiguous (able-and-fluent vs. disengaged-and-guessing) unless gated by engagement — precisely the project SPOV. **[Reasoned inference]**

## Q2 — Is "game-based" a distinct construct, or a delivery layer?

**Delivery layer, confirmed. [Verified finding].** The assessment literature is explicit that games are a *vehicle* wrapping pre-defined constructs, not constructs themselves. Stealth assessment embeds Evidence-Centered Design (ECD) models "directly and invisibly into the fabric of the gaming environment"; the **competency model** defines the construct (e.g., Gf, Gv), the **evidence model** maps observed play to it, and the **task model** is the game situation that elicits evidence. **[Verified finding]** (Shute & Ventura, 2013 [14]; Mislevy, Steinberg, & Almond, 2003 [15]). Applied ECD-for-games work makes the separation operational: the construct lives in the competency model while game mechanics are task-model machinery scored via evidence rules. **[Verified finding]** (Kim, Almond, & Shute, 2016 [16]; DiCerbo, 2014 [17]). A "game-based" domain would therefore be a category error — it names *how* we measure, not *what*. **[Reasoned inference]**

**Construct-irrelevant variance (CIV) from games.** Wrapping constructs in games adds measurement-method variance. A meta-analysis of game-related vs. traditional cognitive measures (52 samples, 44 papers, >6,100 adults) found only a moderate association (observed r ≈ .30; corrected ≈ .45), leaving substantial variance that "raises questions about whether GRAs and traditional measures capture the same aspects of cognitive ability or if GRAs also measure other constructs." **[Verified finding]** (Bipp, Wee, Walczok, & Hansal, 2024 [18]). Prior video-game experience is a concrete CIV source: game usage was *positively* associated with game-based-assessment performance but *negatively* with academic performance — evidence of test bias favoring experienced gamers. **[Verified finding]** (Ohlms, Hohner, & Melchers, 2025 [19]). Time-pressure and elaborate mechanics are the features most likely to shift strategy and measure gaming skill rather than the target ability. **[Verified finding]** (Bipp et al., 2024 [18]).

**Mitigation (already in the design).** A **wordless self-teach demo + warm-up** attacks the two biggest CIV sources for K-8: it removes reading/instruction-language load and equalizes game/device familiarity before any scored item, so scores reflect the construct rather than "did the child already know this genre." **[Reasoned inference]** Keeping mechanics simple and hotkey-uniform, and gating on engagement, further limits motor/device CIV. **[Reasoned inference]** This does not make games a construct; it makes them a *fairer wrapper*. **[Reasoned inference]**

## Decision + rationale

**Choose B.** Cut dedicated PS types; represent speed only as cross-cutting, engagement-gated efficiency + consistency signals; no standalone PS domain and no PS item types. "Game-based" stays a delivery layer.

Rationale, tied to evidence:

1. **Gs is the wrong construct at the top.** Lowest g-loading of the relevant CHC abilities [1]; the lowest, near-irrelevant index in gifted profiles [2][3][4]; and when it is low in gifted kids it is usually an artifact [5]. A dedicated clerical-speed family cannot improve identification of children who can accelerate. **[Reasoned inference]**
2. **The one pro-speed result doesn't transfer.** Incremental validity of PS for general achievement [6] is neither a high-tail nor an acceleration result; at high IQ it's reasoning, not clerical speed, that differentiates [7]. *Correlation/predictive validity ≠ incremental validity for gifted selection.*
3. **Equity (H4) and D-015 raise the cost of PS types.** Timed clerical tasks depress scores for 2e/ADHD/anxious/motor children and can block access [3][8][9]; a no-human screener cannot clinically discount a spuriously low PSI, so the harm is unmitigated. **[Reasoned inference]**
4. **The legitimate speed signal is already free.** Worst-performance/RT-variability [11][12] and mental-speed–g effects [10] live *inside the reasoning items*; capture them as `M-RTVAR`/`M-LAPSE`/`M-SPEEDACC` rather than building clerical tasks. And because "fast" can mean disengaged guessing [13], speed must sit **behind the `M-ENGAGE` gate** — exactly the SPOV.
5. **Game-based is not a domain** [14][15][16][17], and games add CIV [18][19] — so a `game-based` (or `processing_speed`) area would misrepresent the construct space.

This is not a rubber-stamp of the recat: the counter-evidence [10][11][12] is real, and it *would* justify a probe if the probe added unique predictive power. It doesn't here — the same information is obtainable, more fairly and more cheaply, from the reasoning items already being delivered. The candidate types (`PS-BLINK-06`, `PS-DEADLINE-01`, `PS-DECODE-03`, `PS-DIFFSPOT-01`) are textbook clerical/perceptual-speed tasks — precisely the low-g, equity-exposed format the evidence warns against — so option A is not supported.

## What would change the decision

Re-add a minimal PS probe if **[Open assumption]** any of these are shown in the target K-8 population:

- **Incremental Timeback prediction:** a hotkey speed probe predicts acceleration/mastery-pace on the platform *beyond* reasoning scores **and** engagement-gated consistency (`M-SPEEDACC`/`M-RTVAR`) — real incremental validity, not raw correlation.
- **No disparate impact:** the probe does not lower identification for 2e/ADHD/motor/anxious subgroups relative to the reasoning-only model (equity guard for H4).
- **Cross-cutting signals prove insufficient:** `M-RT`/`M-RTVAR`/`M-LAPSE` fail reliability or fail to recover the worst-performance signal, so a purpose-built consistency probe is needed.
- **Fluency need emerges:** automaticity of *learned* skills (CHC's post-learning role of Gs) turns out to matter for Timeback and is better captured as *within-domain* fluency (e.g., quantitative) than as generic clerical speed — which would still be a domain-folded measurement, not a PS domain.

## Catalog action

For the orchestrator to apply (not applied here):

1. **Areas:** Keep the four testable areas only — `fluid_reasoning`, `verbal`, `quantitative`, `spatial`. Do **not** add `processing_speed` as an area. Do **not** add `game_based` as an area.
2. **PS types — cut / keep-cut:** Do not include `PS-BLINK-06` (Blink Towers), `PS-DEADLINE-01` (Beat the Bar), `PS-DECODE-03` (Secret Decoder), `PS-DIFFSPOT-01` (Difference Detectives) as standalone types. If present in the catalog, mark them `deprecated`/removed with reason "clerical Gs, low-g, equity risk (H4), superseded by cross-cutting speed measurements"; do not restore.
3. **Game-based types:** Keep `GB-*` as *delivery wrappers*, each mapped to the underlying construct area it measures (`fluid_reasoning`/`verbal`/`quantitative`/`spatial`) via `delivery: game`. `game-based` is never a `type_area`.
4. **Speed as measurements only:** Represent speed exclusively as cross-cutting measurements attached to existing (reasoning) item types: `M-RT`, `M-COMBO`, `M-SPEEDACC` — each **gated by `M-ENGAGE`** — plus consistency signals `M-RTVAR` and `M-LAPSE`. Scoring must weight raw speed/efficiency **only behind the engagement gate**: speed contributes to ability estimates solely when `M-ENGAGE` indicates solution behavior (not rapid-guessing); fast-but-disengaged responding is discounted, and the worst-performance/lapse structure is retained as an ability signal.
5. **No new item types** are required by this memo.
