How to use — Shape Morph

The top-left shape changes into the top-right shape. Make the bottom shape change in the same way: tap the tile from the tray that belongs in the empty box, then press the check (✓) to send your answer. You can also press number keys 1–4. The machine notes your answer and brings the next puzzle.
