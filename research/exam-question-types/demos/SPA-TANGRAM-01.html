How to use — Shape-Fill Form Board

Watch the pointer solve one example, then try one practice item (not scored). Tap a tray piece, then tap a square to place it (use rotate (R) to turn it) and fill the whole board with no gaps - some tray pieces are extra and will not fit. Press done (✓) to submit. It gets harder each time you fill it exactly.
