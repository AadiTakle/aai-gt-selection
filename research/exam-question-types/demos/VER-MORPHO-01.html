How to use — Word Machines

One picture is named for you, so you know what its word means on its own. Little word-parts get added on the end, and each one changes the picture in its own way. Tap the one answer out of four that matches. One tap sends your answer. Nobody tells you what the word-parts mean — you work that out by watching what the words do.
