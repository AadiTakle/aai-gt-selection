How to use — What Do They See

The map shows a place from above. People stand around it, and each one has an arrow showing the way they are looking. Either tap the person who sees the strip of things shown at the top, or turn the dial to point at the place named on screen from where the marked person stands. Press the check (✓) to send your answer.
