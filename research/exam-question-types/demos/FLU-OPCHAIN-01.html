How to use — Machine Chain

The machine holds some parts. It puts the figure on the left through each part in turn, left to right, and something comes out. Tap the one figure out of five that the machine makes. One tap sends your answer. Nobody tells you what the parts do — you work that out by watching what the machine makes.
