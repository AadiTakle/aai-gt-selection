How to use — Build the Tile

One cell of the grid is empty. Build the tile that belongs there: tap one choice in each row of buttons below (shape, color, how many). Your tile appears in the empty cell as you build it, and you can change any choice. When it looks right, press the check (✓) to send it. The machine takes your tile and brings the next grid.
