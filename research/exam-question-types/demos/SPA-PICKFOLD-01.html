How to use — Which Fold Made It?

The top shows a sheet after it was folded, cut, and unfolded. Tap each choice to watch it fold and cut, then pick the fold order that makes that exact pattern and press the check (✓) to submit. Number keys 1–4 also work. It gets harder each time you are right.
