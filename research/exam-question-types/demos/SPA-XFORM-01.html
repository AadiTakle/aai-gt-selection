How to use — Transform Machine

The machine holds some parts. It puts the pattern on the left through each part in turn, left to right, and a new pattern comes out. Tap the one pattern out of five that the machine makes. One tap sends your answer. Nobody tells you what the parts do — you work that out by watching what the machine makes.
