How to use — Letter Climb

Tap one letter in your word, then tap a new letter to change it. That makes the next rung of your ladder. Keep changing one letter at a time until your word matches the goal word at the top, using as few steps as you can. Press the check (✓) to send your ladder. Use undo (↶) to take a rung back.
