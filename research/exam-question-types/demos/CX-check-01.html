How to use — Check It Twice

Some tiles are in the wrong group. Tap a tile to pick it up, then tap the correct group box (the one whose header pattern it matches) to move it. Tap the tile's check mark once you have reviewed it, then press the check (✓) to turn in. It gets harder each time you fix them all.
