# Measurement Registry

20 measurements. Canonical source of truth is `measurements.json`; this file is generated. Each question type references these IDs and may propose additions (merged here).

| ID | Name | What | Tail-precision usefulness | How to collect | How much |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-ACC | Item accuracy | Correct / incorrect (dichotomous) on a scored item. | The base IRT signal; at the tail, accuracy on very hard items is the primary separator once difficulty is high enough to avoid ceiling. | Log final response vs key per scored item. | >=8-12 scored items per construct per child near the child's ability for a stable theta; more hard items near the cut. |
| M-POLY | Partial-credit (polytomous) score | Graded score for multi-step / multi-part items (0..k). | Extracts tail information from a single rich item (a partial-credit item behaves like several dichotomous ones), sharpening theta with fewer items. | Define ordered scoring categories per item; log category reached. | >=4-6 polytomous items per construct; each contributes more information than one dichotomous item. |
| M-RT | Response time (total) | Milliseconds from item onset to submitted answer. | Combined with accuracy it separates fast-accurate (able) from slow-accurate; feeds effort-moderated and joint speed-accuracy models. | Timestamp item render and submit; store ms. | >=20 RT samples per child for a usable per-child RT distribution; log on every item. |
| M-RTFIRST | First-action latency | Milliseconds from item onset to the child's first interaction. | Indexes planning/encoding before acting; high-ability reflective solvers often show longer first-action latency with fewer revisions. | Timestamp first pointer/key/drag event. | Every interactive item; >=15 samples for a stable per-child median. |
| M-RTVAR | Intra-individual RT variability | SD or coefficient of variation of RT across items. | Consistency (low variability) tracks ability better than peak speed (worst-performance rule); a strong, less-coachable tail signal. | Compute SD/CV over the child's RT series (correct trials). | >=20-30 RT samples for a reliable CV; do NOT reward raw speed. |
| M-LAPSE | Lapse rate | Frequency / magnitude of the child's slowest responses (worst-performance). | The slowest responses, not the fastest, are most diagnostic of ability; fewer lapses => higher ability. | Take the slowest quartile/decile of RTs; count lapses beyond a threshold. | >=25 trials so the slow tail of the RT distribution is estimable. |
| M-REV | Revisions / answer changes | Count of answer changes, undos, or re-drags before commit. | Distinguishes confident tail performers (few, targeted revisions) from guess-and-check; adds process variance beyond right/wrong. | Increment a counter on each answer change / undo event. | Every item; interpret relative to item difficulty. |
| M-PATH | Solution path / move sequence | Ordered sequence of actions taken to reach the answer. | Strategy signature: efficient/novel paths mark strong reasoners; supports evidence-model (Bayes-net) scoring that separates HOW, not just WHETHER. | Append each action {t, action, state} to an event log per item. | Log fully on constructed/interactive items; needs >=5 such items per construct to characterize strategy. |
| M-EFF | Efficiency vs optimal | Moves or time used relative to the optimal solution for the item's difficulty. | Near-optimal solving at high difficulty is a clean tail separator; catches able children who are also economical. | Compare M-PATH length/time to a stored optimal for the item. | >=5 items with known optima per construct. |
| M-HINT | Scaffold / hint usage | Count and timing of hints or scaffolds the child invokes. | Hint-free solving at difficulty is stronger evidence; dynamic-assessment signal (learning rate) can add incremental tail validity. | Log each hint request with timestamp and item state. | Every item that offers hints; interpret against difficulty. |
| M-PERSIST | Persistence / time-on-task | Time spent engaging a hard item before solving or giving up. | Task commitment (Renzulli) is a construct CogAT misses; persistence on hard items adds non-cognitive tail signal and flags disengagement. | Time from onset to solve/skip on items above the child's level. | >=6 above-level items per child. |
| M-ERRTYPE | Error type | Classification of errors: near-miss / systematic / random / motor-slip. | Near-misses at high difficulty indicate latent ability just below threshold, refining tail placement; filters motor/attention noise. | Tag the chosen wrong answer against a distractor taxonomy. | Every incorrect response; needs distractors designed with error meaning. |
| M-CONF | Confidence / wager | Elicited confidence rating or points wager on the answer. | Metacognitive calibration (accuracy of confidence) adds incremental validity and helps down-weight lucky guesses at the tail. | Optional quick confidence tap or wager after answering. | On a subset (>=8 items) to avoid burden; not every item. |
| M-EXPLORE | Exploration behavior | Number of options sampled / states explored before committing. | Systematic exploration vs flailing separates strategic tail solvers; useful in open/interactive items. | Count distinct states/options visited via M-PATH log. | Interactive items only; >=5 per construct. |
| M-PROG | Progress trajectory | Partial progress achieved over a multi-step item over time. | Turns one hard item into a curve; the shape (fast-to-plateau vs steady) adds tail resolution beyond final score. | Snapshot state/score at intervals or per sub-step. | Multi-step items; >=4 per construct. |
| M-SPEEDACC | Speed-accuracy tradeoff | Joint position on the RT x accuracy plane. | Models the child's chosen operating point so speed is only credited when accuracy is held; prevents rewarding impulsive fast-wrong responding. | Pair M-RT with M-ACC per item; fit per-child SAT. | >=20 items with both signals. |
| M-DIFFREACH | Ceiling / max difficulty reached | Highest item difficulty answered correctly. | Directly probes the tail; adaptive escalation to failure pinpoints the child's edge and is the sharpest single tail statistic. | Track max b-parameter solved under adaptive escalation. | Requires an item bank extending well above grade level (above-level items). |
| M-DRIFT | Performance drift (fatigue/engagement) | Change in accuracy/RT across the session. | Detects disengagement/fatigue that would bias a tail decision; lets scoring discount low-effort late items. | Regress accuracy/RT on item position within session. | Whole session; needs >=30 items to estimate a slope. |
| M-ENGAGE | Engagement / off-task | Idle time, focus loss (blur), and re-engagement latency. | Gates all other signals: a tail decision is only valid on engaged responses (supports the 'speed as evidence only when engaged' principle). | Log window blur/idle timers and time-to-resume. | Continuous background logging across the session. |
| M-CONSIST | Cross-item consistency | Reliability of responses across parallel/equivalent items. | Reduces conditional standard error at the cut; inconsistent responders near the cut get more items before a decision. | Compare responses to matched parallel items (same b, cloned via AIG). | >=3 matched pairs near the child's ability. |
